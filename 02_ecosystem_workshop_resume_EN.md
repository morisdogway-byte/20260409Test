### [Mapping] Draw the lines of connection

Connect each person to "Me" using arrows and symbols that represent the type of relationship.

**Symbol guide:**

| Symbol | Meaning | How to draw |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| `←→` | Complementary (giving and receiving mutually) | Double-headed arrow |
| `→→` | Predatory / competitive (a power imbalance) | Single bold arrow |
| `⇢` | Parasitic (one-directional, draining) | Dotted arrow |
| `—` | Coexistence (healthy distance, neutral) | Straight line |
| `〜〜` | Seasonal (the relationship shifts over time) | Wavy line |

> **Arrow direction:** points in the direction energy, support, or influence flows  
> **Distance from center:** the closer to "Me," the deeper the relationship

## Reference: Relationship Type Cheat Sheet

| Type | Self | Other | Example in nature | Keywords |
|------|------|-------|-------------------|----------|
| **Complementary (Mutualism)** | + | + | Bees & flowers, fungi & trees | Giving, co-growth |
| **Predatory (Predation / Competition)** | + | − | Lions & zebras | Power, competition, pressure |
| **Parasitic (One-directional)** | − | + | Fleas, brood parasitism, mistletoe | Draining, dependency, one-way |
| **Coexistence (Neutral / Commensal)** | 0 | 0 | Mixed-diet birds, coral reefs | Distance, niche, diversity |

> + = benefit     − = harm     0 = no significant effect

## Reference: Five Perspectives — Quick Guide

| Perspective | Concept in nature | Question for your relationships |
|-------------|-------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Seasonality** | Winter trees stay connected underground | What season is this relationship in right now? |
| **Edge Effect** | Boundaries generate the richest diversity | Who lives at the edge of your comfort zone? |
| **Decomposer** | Death becomes nourishment for new life | What have you received from what has ended? |
| **Inner ecosystem** | 100 trillion microbes coexist within you | How are your inner "voices" coexisting? |
| **Keystone species** | One species can hold the whole system in balance | Who — or what — is your keystone? |
